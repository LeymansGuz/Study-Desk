Data use and study terms

App updates

Reuses unchanged update blocks when a verified cached package is available. Otherwise downloads the full signed package from GitHub. Your study data is kept separately. Installation requires an idle app and a verified backup.

AI connection

Use your own API key. Each provider’s key is saved separately in your system credential store and excluded from backups. Leave the key blank when switching back to a saved provider. AI requests send relevant course excerpts to the selected provider.

Get an OpenRouter key

Advanced model settings

OpenAI-compatible endpoints vary. Reasoning tokens consume the response budget; mandatory-reasoning models can be slower or incompatible. Models not tested with Study Desk may fail its strict validation. Free-only manual selection requires a matching refreshed catalog entry.

Paid models can charge your provider account. Check model and hosting-provider terms and pricing before saving.

Local maintenance

Manage your AI provider and key above. Run Setup.cmd for source dependencies. Use Start.cmd to open the workspace and Stop.cmd to stop its local services. AI requests send relevant document excerpts to your provider.
